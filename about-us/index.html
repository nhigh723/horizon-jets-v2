About

About Horizon Jets

Who we are

Horizon Jets is a small charter brokerage, built to make private jet travel straightforward. The industry talks to first-time charter customers in jargon or sales patter — rarely in plain English, and rarely with the customer's interests first. We started Horizon Jets to do it differently: straight answers, honest pricing, and one person handling your trip from the first quote to wheels down.

There is no big company behind this site. There is no fleet, no sales team, and no call center — and that's the point. You work directly with the person arranging your flight. No hand-offs, no runaround, no wondering who has your booking.

What we do

  • Source real quotes for your trip from licensed FAA Part 135 operators — the companies that own and fly the aircraft.
  • Arrange your booking once you've chosen a flight: confirm the aircraft, the operator, and the terms with you before anything is final.
  • Explain charter plainly — costs, aircraft types, airports, empty legs — so you know what you're buying.
  • Help frequent flyers compare providers' jet cards, if a card fits how they fly.

What we don't do

  • We don't operate aircraft. We own no jets and employ no pilots. We're a broker, not a carrier.
  • We don't run a jet card program. We help you evaluate providers' cards; we don't sell our own.
  • We don't sell aircraft.
  • We don't offer instant booking or guaranteed availability. Every flight is quoted for your exact trip.
  • We don't publish reviews or testimonials we can't verify.

Broker disclosure

Horizon Jets is an air charter broker, not a direct air carrier. All flights are operated by licensed FAA Part 135 operators. The operator flying your trip is disclosed to you before you book.

How we get paid

When you book a flight we arrange, we earn a fee from the booking. Your quote shows the full price of your trip — the aircraft, the schedule, and every charge broken down — so what you see is what you pay. And because we don't own aircraft or sell a program, we're not steering you toward any particular jet: the quote that fits your trip is the one we recommend.

Why a small brokerage?

Fair question. The honest answer is: check the work. The cost figures on this site are typical ranges from published charter cost guides, and we say so wherever they appear. Where we don't know something, we say that too. If you spot an error, tell us through the contact page and we'll fix it.
