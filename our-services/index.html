Services

What we do

Horizon Jets is a charter brokerage. Here's everything we do for your trip — and, just as importantly, what we don't do.

On-demand charter

Tell us about your trip — where you're going, when, and how many people are flying — and we'll source quotes from licensed FAA Part 135 operators for your exact flight. You get the aircraft, the operator, the schedule, and the full price broken down. When you've chosen, we arrange the booking and confirm everything with you before it's final.

There is no obligation to book, and no payment is taken through this site. Charter is priced per flight, not per seat, and every quote is built around your specific route and dates.

Quote sourcing & comparison

Charter pricing moves with availability, positioning, and timing — two quotes for the same route can differ by thousands of dollars. We source quotes from licensed operators for your trip and lay them out side by side, so you can compare aircraft, schedule, and price before you commit.

Empty-leg requests

An empty leg is a repositioning flight — a jet flying empty to its next job — sold at a discount, often 25–75% below standard charter rates. If your dates are flexible, ask us to check for an empty leg on your route when you request a quote. We'll look at what's actually available for your dates and tell you straight whether an empty leg fits — or whether a standard charter is the better move.

We don't list live empty legs and have no inventory feed. Our empty-leg guide explains how they work, what they actually cost, and the catches: fixed routes and times, short booking windows, and the chance the flight disappears if the original trip changes.

Jet-card guidance

Flying 25 or more hours a year? A jet card — prepaid hours at a fixed rate — might fit better than booking trip by trip. We don't run a jet card program, but we can help you compare providers' cards: the rates, the terms, the catches, and whether a card actually saves you money at your flying volume.

How jet cards work

Aircraft comparison guide

Light jet or midsize? Turboprop or heavy jet? Our comparison guide lays out the aircraft categories side by side — typical seating, range, and hourly cost — so you can see which type fits your trip before you request a quote.

The guide covers published specifications and typical cost ranges. It is not a live availability tool: it can't tell you which specific aircraft are free on a given day. Only a quote for your exact trip can do that.

What we don't do

  • We don't operate aircraft. Horizon Jets owns no jets and employs no pilots. We're a broker, not a carrier — flights are operated by licensed FAA Part 135 operators.
  • We don't run a jet card program. We help you evaluate providers' cards; we don't sell our own.
  • We don't sell aircraft. We don't broker aircraft sales or acquisitions.
  • We don't offer instant booking. Charter pricing depends on live availability, so every flight is quoted individually.

If you were looking for one of those things, the quote form is still the right place to start — we'll point you in the right direction.
