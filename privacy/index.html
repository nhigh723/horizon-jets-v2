Privacy policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

Who we are

Horizon Jets is an air charter broker, not a direct air carrier. All flights are operated by licensed FAA Part 135 operators. The operator flying your trip is disclosed to you before you book. This policy describes what this website collects and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you use the quote form, we collect the trip details you enter (departure, destination, dates, passenger count, and any notes) and your contact information (name, email, and phone number if you provide one). When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message. We do not collect payment information — no payment is taken through this site.

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies. The range map loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap; your browser connects to OpenStreetMap's tile servers when you view that page, as it does for any embedded map.

How we use it

We use your information only to arrange your quote request or to reply to your message. To price your trip, we share the trip details and your contact information with the licensed operators we ask to quote it — they need the route, dates, and passenger count to price the flight, and a way to reach you through us. We do not sell your information to anyone, and we do not use it for marketing you didn't ask for.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and messages for as long as needed to handle your trip or answer your message, and then delete them on request. Email us at Sales@HorizonJets.com to ask what we hold about you or to have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
