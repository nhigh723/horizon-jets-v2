Request a charter quote

Tell us about your trip and we'll source quotes from licensed operators and arrange the booking when you're ready. There is no obligation to book, and no payment is taken through this site.

Horizon Jets is an air charter broker, not a direct air carrier. All flights are operated by licensed FAA Part 135 operators. The operator flying your trip will be named in your quote before you book.

What happens next

1

You send the form below

It takes about two minutes.

2

We source quotes from licensed operators

We check aircraft availability for your route and dates and put together real pricing — the aircraft, the operator, the schedule, and the full price broken down.

3

You choose the flight that fits

Compare the options, ask questions, take your time. If the timing or price doesn't work, there's nothing to cancel and nothing owed.

4

We arrange the booking

Once you've picked a flight, we confirm the aircraft, the operator, and the terms with you — then arrange the booking. Nothing is final until you've seen the full picture and said yes.

A few honest notes before you start

  • There are no instant quotes. Charter pricing depends on which aircraft are actually available on your dates, so every flight is priced individually. Any site promising an exact price on the spot is guessing.
  • Availability is never guaranteed until a flight is booked. The aircraft that fits your trip may already be committed on your dates. We'll tell you what's actually available.
  • Charter is priced per flight, not per seat. One passenger or a full cabin, the aircraft costs the same — which is why the form asks how many people are flying (it decides the size of the aircraft, not the price per person).
  • Your details stay with your quote. We only use your information to arrange your quote request. We don't sell it.
