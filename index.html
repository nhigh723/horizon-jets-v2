Why people charter

Time

You arrive minutes before departure, not hours. No commercial terminals, no connections, no waiting at baggage claim.

Access

Private aircraft can use more than 5,000 airports across the US — far more than the few hundred served by airlines — so you often land much closer to where you're actually going.

Control

The schedule is yours. Leave when you want, change plans when you need to, and fly with only your own group on board.

Privacy

Your travel stays your business. No public passenger lists, no crowds, no overheard conversations.

Charter isn't the right choice for every trip — for a single traveler on a busy route, a first-class commercial seat is often better value. We'll tell you straight if that's the case for yours.

What does chartering actually cost?

Most one-way domestic trips run roughly $7,000 to $65,000 for the whole aircraft, depending on the size of the jet and the length of the flight. The final price is the hourly rate times the flight time, plus taxes, airport fees, and any positioning — the empty flight to get the aircraft to you.

The only way to know your price is a quote for your exact trip. That's what we're here for.
