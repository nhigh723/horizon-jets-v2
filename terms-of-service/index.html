Terms of Service

Last updated: November 15, 2026.

Broker disclosure

Horizon Jets is an air charter broker, not a direct air carrier. All flights are operated by licensed FAA Part 135 operators. Horizon Jets does not own or operate aircraft. The operator flying your trip is disclosed to you before you book. Horizon Jets does not run a jet card program and does not sell aircraft.

Quotes are not bookings

A quote is a price for a specific trip on a specific date, based on aircraft availability at the time it is prepared. A quote is not a booking and not a guarantee of availability or price. Nothing is booked until you have accepted a quote in writing and the operator has confirmed the aircraft. Availability and price can change until the booking is confirmed.

Prices on this site

Hourly rates, cost ranges, and aircraft specifications on this site are reference figures and planning ranges, not quotes. Your actual price depends on the route, the date, the aircraft, positioning, taxes, and fees. Only a quote for your exact trip gives the final price, and the quote will itemize what it includes.

Cancellation and changes

Cancellation and change terms depend on the operator for your specific flight. We will explain the terms that apply before you commit to a booking. Always confirm the cancellation policy in writing before you pay anything.

Payments

No payment is taken through this website. Payment for a booked flight is arranged as part of the booking, under the terms given in your quote and booking confirmation.

Accuracy

We work to keep the information on this site accurate, and we correct errors when we find them. Aircraft specifications and listed rates change over time; if you spot an error, tell us through the contact page and we'll fix it.

Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent through the contact page, by email to Sales@HorizonJets.com, or by phone at 781-772-5248.
